## Cross-site findings

1. The strongest homepages lead with one proposition that can be understood without scrolling.
2. Strategy, design, and technology are usually framed as one connected capability, not three unrelated services.
3. A clear contact action stays visible while work remains available as supporting proof.
4. Typography and one recognizable visual system create more distinction than a grid of generic service cards.
5. Large agencies lean on client logos, awards, and volume of work. Synaptech should not imitate proof it does not yet have.

## Design opportunity for Synaptech

Own the idea of connection already present in the Synaptech mark: purpose at the center, with strategy, design, and development moving together. Pair that visual system with a plainspoken promise, one factual explanatory sentence, one project link, and one contact action. Keep the homepage within one viewport and leave detailed proof to the project pages.

## Evidence boundary

This is a design-focused quick scan of public official homepages. It does not include SEO, pricing, review, traffic, or market-share analysis.
